An end-of-tenancy cleaning quote becomes much more useful when the company understands the property as it is now. Bedroom count alone cannot explain a heavily used kitchen, several bathrooms or carpets that need specialist attention.

Before requesting prices in Derby, gather a short description, recent photographs where appropriate and the date the property will be empty. This gives cleaning companies a common basis for their proposals and helps you compare the work included.

## Describe the layout and current condition

State the number of bedrooms, bathrooms and reception rooms, plus whether the property is furnished. Mention stairs, additional toilets and areas such as a utility room that might otherwise be missed.

Explain the condition honestly. Grease, substantial limescale, pet hair and long-standing residue can change the time required. Photographs should show the relevant surfaces rather than only wide views of tidy rooms.

Remove personal documents from photographs and obtain permission before sharing images of someone else's belongings. A useful assessment does not require a complete record of the occupant's life.

## List the extras explicitly

Ask for appliance interiors, cupboards and carpets by name. Different companies use different definitions of an end-of-tenancy clean, so a familiar service label is not enough.

| Detail to supply | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Oven type and condition | Access, components and cleaning time vary |
| Fridge and freezer | Contents and defrosting affect readiness |
| Cupboard interiors | They need to be empty and accessible |
| Carpeted rooms | Extraction may be separately quoted |
| Internal windows | Access and safe reach determine scope |

Explain whether blinds, upholstery or unusual finishes need attention. Do not assume external windows, high-level work, rubbish removal or repairs are included without agreement.

## Plan the clean around moving day

Cleaning is easier to assess when furniture, boxes and waste have already been removed as agreed. Tell the company when movers finish and when keys must be returned, leaving room for an inspection.

Confirm that water and electricity will remain available. If an appliance needs defrosting, follow its instructions and arrange that preparation in advance rather than expecting it to happen during a short cleaning visit.

Allow appropriate drying time for any carpet work. The actual time depends on the method, material and conditions; ask the provider how this affects access and furniture replacement.

## Compare written scopes

Check each quotation against the same task list. Look at included rooms, appliance interiors, access requirements and any assumptions about condition. Ask how unexpected work is assessed before it is carried out.

A written scope also helps prevent confusion at checkout. Keep the agreed quotation and any revised instructions together so the person inspecting the property understands what was commissioned.

Cleaning can address dirt and residue within the agreed scope, but it does not repair damage or normal wear. A cleaning invoice does not by itself guarantee a deposit outcome.

## Send a useful first enquiry

Include the property postcode, room count, preferred date, empty-property status, key arrangements and photographs of the main problem areas. Add any inventory or checkout requirements that affect the requested work.
