A professional deep clean should be defined by a checklist, not a vague promise that everything will be spotless. Property size, starting condition, access and optional specialist tasks all affect what can be completed.

This room-by-room guide helps you discuss scope before accepting a quote.

## Whole-property tasks

Subject to safe access, a deep clean may include:

- Dusting accessible high and low surfaces
- Removing cobwebs
- Wiping doors, frames and handles
- Cleaning skirting boards and window sills
- Wiping radiators when cool
- Cleaning switches and accessible touchpoints
- Vacuuming carpets, edges and rugs
- Mopping suitable hard floors
- Moving light, safe furniture where agreed
- Emptying ordinary household bins

Heavy furniture, fragile collections and unsafe high-level areas may be excluded. Confirm who moves items before the booking.

## Kitchen

Kitchen deep cleaning often requires significant degreasing:

- Worktops, splashbacks, sink and taps
- Hob surface, controls and pan supports
- Cupboard and drawer fronts
- Accessible cupboard interiors if emptied
- Appliance exteriors and handles
- Microwave interior
- Extractor exterior and removable filters where agreed
- Fridge interior if emptied and included
- Floor edges and accessible areas beneath movable items

An intensive oven clean may be a separately priced specialist service. Ask whether racks, trays, doors and interior panels are included.

## Bathrooms and WCs

Typical detailed tasks include:

- Cleaning and disinfecting toilets
- Descaling taps and suitable fittings
- Cleaning baths, showers, basins and screens
- Removing soap residue
- Cleaning mirrors, shelves and cupboards
- Wiping tile surfaces
- Cleaning accessible extractor covers
- Detail-cleaning floor edges

Cleaning can remove surface contamination and some build-up, but it cannot repair failed sealant, damaged grout, permanent staining or a moisture source causing mould.

## Bedrooms

- Dust clear furniture and ledges
- Wipe wardrobes and drawers externally
- Clean empty interiors where agreed
- Wipe headboards where the material permits
- Vacuum beneath movable beds
- Clean skirting boards, doors and frames
- Vacuum carpets slowly and detail edges
- Mop suitable hard flooring

Changing bedding is normally included only if agreed and fresh linen is provided.

## Living rooms and hallways

- Dust accessible furniture, shelving and ornaments
- Vacuum beneath removable cushions
- Wipe suitable tables and hard surfaces
- Clean internal glass and mirrors where included
- Vacuum upholstered surfaces if agreed
- Detail entrances, stairs and floor edges
- Remove visible marks where safe products allow

Carpet extraction and upholstery wet cleaning need specific equipment and should be stated separately.

## Utility rooms and less-used spaces

Utility areas, porches, conservatories and storage rooms can consume unexpected time. Include them in the room list and describe their condition.

If a garage, balcony or external area is required, ask explicitly. These are not automatically part of an internal deep clean.

## Services often priced separately

Depending on the company, optional work may include:

- Oven cleaning
- Carpet extraction
- Upholstery cleaning
- External windows
- Internal windows throughout
- Wall washing
- Biohazard or sharps handling
- Pest-related cleaning
- Waste clearance
- High-level access work

A general domestic cleaner may not be qualified, equipped or insured for every specialist task.

## What cleaning cannot promise

An honest provider will explain likely limitations. Results may be restricted by:

- Permanent dye or surface damage
- Deep scratches and wear
- Old stains and chemical changes
- Damaged grout or sealant
- Mould linked to unresolved damp
- Burn marks
- Inaccessible areas
- Delicate materials with limited safe treatment options

Photographs help set expectations, but results should not be guaranteed where the surface response is uncertain.

## Information needed for an accurate quote

Provide:

- Property type and approximate size
- Number of bedrooms and bathrooms
- Occupied or empty status
- Current condition
- Pets, smoking or unusual soil
- Parking and access details
- Priority rooms
- Optional appliances, carpets or windows
- Deadline and preferred date

Short videos or photographs can show build-up more clearly than a one-line request.

## Prepare before the team arrives

Declutter surfaces, secure valuables, wash or remove dishes and provide clear access. Identify delicate materials and previous damage. If cupboards should be cleaned internally, empty them first unless packing is part of the quote.
