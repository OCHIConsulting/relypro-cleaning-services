Our Derby service areas

We cover the Derby localities listed below. Appointments depend on the requested service, team capacity, parking and travel time, so share your postcode for a definite availability check.

City Centre, Cathedral Quarter and Riverside

We support apartments, short-stay properties, offices and suitable homes in central Derby. Please mention concierge arrangements, controlled entrances, lifts, loading restrictions and paid parking so access time and costs can be reflected accurately in the quote.

Pride Park, Alvaston and Rose Hill

Customers and property managers can request Airbnb turnovers, regular or deep cleaning, moving-related work and suitable commercial visits. For apartments and managed buildings, share the entry process, service window and parking arrangements in advance.

Allestree, Oakwood, Chaddesden, Spondon and Darley Abbey

Our coverage includes domestic cleaning, carpets, ovens, end-of-tenancy work and selected recurring workplace arrangements. Tell us about pets, delicate flooring, restricted access or products that must not be used before the appointment is confirmed.

Mickleover and Littleover

We provide regular home cleaning, detailed one-off visits, end-of-tenancy work and selected specialist services. The bedroom and bathroom count, current condition and any parking restrictions help us estimate a realistic duration and price.

Sinfin and Chellaston

Customers can request routine, deep and moving-related cleans, as well as suitable office work. Flats and shared-access buildings may need additional information about entry systems, lifts, stairs and where equipment can be unloaded.

Not sure whether your address is covered? Send the postcode and requested service. We will confirm availability, travel and any parking considerations before you decide whether to proceed.
