What a RelyPro deep clean covers

A deep clean is designed for properties that have fallen behind, need a seasonal reset or require detailed attention before guests, an event or a change in routine. We agree the priorities before arrival so time is spent on the areas that matter most.

Kitchen detail

Worktops, cupboard fronts, splashbacks, sinks, appliance exteriors and reachable high-touch areas. Inside cupboards or appliances can be added to the checklist.

Bathrooms

Taps, showers, screens, baths, tiles, sanitaryware and accessible fittings, with attention to soap residue, limescale and built-up marks.

Living areas

Detailed dusting, skirting boards, reachable ledges, doors, handles, switches, vacuuming and mopping appropriate to the surface.

Bedrooms and circulation

Surfaces, mirrors, accessible furniture exteriors, floors, stairs, banisters and the small details that routine visits may not cover.

Before we confirm your price

We ask about the property size, current condition, access, parking, pets and any specialist surfaces. This avoids unrealistic fixed prices and lets us recommend the right amount of time. Heavy waste removal, hazardous materials, pest contamination and specialist restoration are not included unless agreed separately.

Guide price: from £25 per cleaner-hour with a four-hour minimum booking. Your written quote confirms the agreed checklist, estimated duration and any optional extras before work begins.
