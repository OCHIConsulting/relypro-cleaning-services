A process matched to the carpet

Good carpet cleaning starts with the fibre, backing, construction, age and condition—not simply the visible mark. We ask for room sizes and photos, then check suitability before choosing a method. Delicate rugs, specialist fibres and unstable dyes may need a specialist assessment.

Pre-inspection

We identify higher-wear routes, staining, edges and furniture considerations, and discuss anything that may limit the outcome.

Preparation

Accessible areas are vacuumed and suitable spots are pre-treated. Customers should move small or fragile items before arrival.

Cleaning

The chosen process targets general soil and suitable stains while managing moisture and protecting adjacent surfaces.

Drying guidance

We explain ventilation, safe access and likely drying conditions. Drying time varies with fibre, airflow, humidity and temperature.

Realistic stain expectations

Some colour changes are permanent or caused by bleaching, wear, dye transfer or damage rather than removable soil. We cannot promise removal of every mark, but we will set expectations before treatment where the limitation is visible.
