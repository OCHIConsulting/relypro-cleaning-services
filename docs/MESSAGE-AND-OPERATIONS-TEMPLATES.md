# Human-approved message and operating templates

Replace bracketed fields from the CRM, review each message, and record the sent contact. Do not add recipients to marketing.

## Acknowledgement

Subject: We received your RelyPro enquiry [reference]

Hi [first name], thank you for contacting RelyPro about [service]. Your reference is [reference]. A team member will review the details and reply [approved response window]. If anything is time-sensitive, reply to this message or call [business phone].

Domestic tone: warm and practical. Airbnb tone: mention the requested turnaround/property detail. Commercial tone: offer a short requirements call or site assessment.

## Requirements clarification

Hi [first name], to prepare an accurate [service] quote, could you confirm [two or three specific missing facts]? Please avoid sending access codes or other sensitive information at this stage.

## Quote cover note

Hi [first name], attached/below is RelyPro's quote for [scope]. It covers [included items] and notes [assumptions/exclusions]. Please review the proposed date and scope; nothing is booked until we confirm in writing.

## Follow-up one (two working days after quote)

Hi [first name], I’m checking that you received quote [reference] for [service]. If you would like anything clarified or adjusted, reply here and we’ll review it with you.

## Follow-up two (seven days, final)

Hi [first name], one final check-in about quote [reference]. If the timing is no longer right, no action is needed and we’ll close the enquiry. You’re welcome to contact us again when plans change.

## Satisfaction and recovery

Satisfaction: Hi [first name], how did today’s [service] go? Please reply with anything you would like us to review.

Complaint recovery: Thank you for telling us. I’m sorry the service did not meet the expected standard. I have recorded the issue under [reference]. [owner] will review [specific concern] and contact you by [time]. We will not request a review while this is unresolved.

## Review request

Hi [first name], thank you for confirming you were happy with the service. If you’re comfortable doing so, would you share an honest Google review here: [verified Google review link]? Please mention only details you are happy to make public.

## Case study template

- Customer category and Derby area (only with permission)
- Challenge and required outcome
- Agreed scope and exclusions
- Delivery controls/checklist
- Measurable or attributable outcome
- Approved testimonial, exact wording, permission date, and permitted channels
- Genuine image consent and metadata-removal check
- Relevant call to action

## Daily operating checklist

1. Review New enquiry records and assign an owner.
2. Check duplicates by submission ID, reference, and contact details before merging.
3. Set the next action/date after every contact.
4. Move stages only when the stated milestone occurred.
5. Record sent messages and opt-outs; never infer marketing consent.
6. Reconcile booked/completed work and revenue weekly.
7. Close stale/lost leads with a controlled reason.
8. Delete or anonymise records according to the published retention rule.
