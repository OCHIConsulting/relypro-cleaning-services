“Deep clean” is used so widely that two quotes with the same label can describe very different jobs. The clearest way to compare services is to look at the starting condition, the agreed checklist and the time allocated.

A regular clean maintains an already manageable home. A deep clean tackles accumulated grime and areas that are not normally included in a routine visit.

## What a regular clean is designed to do

Regular cleaning keeps frequently used rooms comfortable from week to week. A typical visit may include:

- Dusting accessible surfaces
- Cleaning kitchen worktops, sink and hob surface
- Cleaning toilets, basins, baths or showers
- Emptying household bins
- Vacuuming carpets and rugs
- Mopping suitable hard floors
- Wiping obvious marks from touchpoints
- General straightening where agreed

Because the cleaner returns on a predictable schedule, the focus is maintenance. It does not normally allow enough time to remove heavy limescale, detail every cupboard front or clean behind large furniture.

## What makes a deep clean different

A deep clean allocates time to details and build-up. The exact scope varies, but it may include:

- Detailed degreasing in the kitchen
- Cleaning cupboard fronts, handles and accessible interiors
- Removing heavier soap residue and limescale
- Wiping doors, frames, skirting boards and radiators
- Cleaning reachable high and low-level surfaces
- Vacuuming edges and beneath movable furniture
- Detailed dusting of ornaments, shelving and ledges
- Cleaning internal windows where agreed
- Treating selected problem areas with suitable products

Optional services such as oven cleaning, carpet extraction or exterior windows should be stated separately. Do not assume they are included merely because the service is called a deep clean.

## Choose a regular clean when

Regular cleaning is usually the better fit when:

- The home is already in reasonable condition
- You want weekly or fortnightly support
- Kitchens and bathrooms receive routine attention
- Clutter is managed before the cleaner arrives
- You want to prevent gradual build-up
- The same core tasks matter at each visit

It is often cost-effective to begin with a deeper initial clean and then maintain the result with shorter recurring visits.

## Choose a deep clean when

Consider a deep clean if:

- The property has not been professionally cleaned for a long time
- Grease, dust or limescale has accumulated
- You are preparing for guests or a special event
- A house move or renovation has disrupted normal routines
- You are starting a recurring cleaning service
- Specific rooms need a thorough reset
- You want seasonal attention to neglected areas

Some properties need more than one visit. Heavy soiling, large floor areas and numerous detailed items can make a staged plan more realistic than promising everything in a short booking.

## Deep cleaning is not specialist restoration

A cleaner should be honest about what ordinary cleaning can achieve. Permanent staining, damaged sealant, worn surfaces, mould caused by an unresolved moisture problem and ingrained discolouration may require repair or a specialist contractor.

Similarly, cleaners should not move unsafe appliances, work at unprotected height or use unsuitable chemicals in an attempt to meet an unrealistic result.

The best quote distinguishes between:

- Dirt that should respond to cleaning
- Stains that may improve but cannot be guaranteed
- Damage or deterioration that cleaning will not correct
- Areas that are inaccessible or unsafe

## How to compare deep-cleaning quotes

Price alone tells you very little unless the scope is comparable. Ask each provider:

1. Which rooms and tasks are included?
2. How many cleaner-hours are allowed?
3. Are products and equipment supplied?
4. Are oven, carpet or internal-window services extra?
5. What needs to be moved or decluttered beforehand?
6. How are access, parking and keys handled?
7. What happens if the property requires more time than expected?

Photographs can help with an initial estimate, but a larger or unusual property may require a visit.

## Prepare for a successful deep clean

Remove clutter, wash up dishes and identify priority areas before the team arrives. Secure valuables, medicines and confidential paperwork. Ensure there is safe access to water, electricity and the rooms included in the quote.

If you have delicate stone, wood, specialist appliances or known surface damage, mention it in advance. Product choice should suit the material rather than relying on one strong chemical everywhere.
