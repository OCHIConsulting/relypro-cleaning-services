Choosing a cleaner involves trust as well as price. Someone may hold keys, enter an occupied home or represent your business before staff and guests arrive.

The right company should make the scope, price, access process and limitations understandable before the first clean.

## Start with the service you actually need

Define whether you want:

- Recurring home cleaning
- A one-off deep clean
- End-of-tenancy cleaning
- Airbnb turnovers and restocking
- Office or communal-area cleaning
- Carpet cleaning

A provider that performs several services may still have different teams, equipment and availability for each. Ask about the specific work rather than assuming all cleaning experience is interchangeable.

## Compare written scopes, not labels

“Standard”, “deep” and “end-of-tenancy” are not universal specifications. Request a checklist stating:

- Rooms covered
- Tasks included
- Tasks excluded
- Optional extras
- Cleaner-hours or expected duration where relevant
- Products and equipment supplied
- Access and parking assumptions
- Final price or pricing basis

If two quotes differ greatly, compare the allocated work before concluding that one is cheaper.

## Check business and insurance information

Ask for the business’s:

- Trading name and contact details
- Service area
- Relevant insurance confirmation
- Process for accidental damage
- Invoice or receipt arrangements
- Privacy information if personal data is collected

For commercial work, you may also need risk assessments, product information or site-specific documentation.

## Ask how cleaners are selected and supported

Useful questions include:

- Are cleaners employees, workers or subcontractors?
- How is suitability assessed?
- What training or induction is provided?
- Who supervises standards?
- Is cover available for illness or holidays?
- How are keys and alarm details controlled?

The company should be able to explain its process without disclosing private employee information.

## Look for specific, believable evidence

Reviews are useful when they describe the service, property type and communication. Read a range rather than only the highest-rated comments.

Be cautious with:

- Guarantees that ignore stated conditions
- Unverifiable awards or accreditations
- Stock images presented as completed local work
- Prices that omit minimum bookings or major extras
- Claims that every stain or deposit return is guaranteed

Responsible providers explain both what they do and what cleaning cannot fix.

## Test the communication before booking

The quotation process gives an early indication of service quality. Notice whether the company:

- Asks relevant questions
- Responds clearly
- Confirms the date and arrival window
- Records your priorities
- Explains payment terms
- Identifies optional work
- Provides a way to report concerns

A quote based on only “How many bedrooms?” may miss bathrooms, property condition, access, pets, floor types or a tight deadline.

## Understand how pricing works

Common approaches include:

- Hourly or cleaner-hour pricing
- Fixed price for a defined checklist
- Per-room carpet pricing
- Contract pricing after an office assessment
- Property-specific Airbnb turnover rates

None is automatically better. The important point is knowing what changes the final charge and what happens if the condition differs from the description.

## Clarify cancellations and re-clean arrangements

Before paying, read:

- Deposit requirements
- Cancellation and rescheduling notice
- Access-failure charges
- Satisfaction or re-clean conditions
- Time limit for reporting issues
- Excluded results such as permanent stains or damage

Photograph and report any concern promptly. A reasonable re-clean process should be precise rather than an unlimited guarantee.

## Consider local fit

A Derby cleaning company should be transparent about the neighbourhoods it covers and any travel or parking constraints. Check the current [RelyPro service areas](/areas.html) and provide the full postcode when requesting a quote.

Local availability matters particularly for same-day Airbnb turnovers and office access windows. Reliability is more valuable than an unrealistically broad promise to serve everywhere at any time.

## Use a final comparison checklist

Before booking, confirm:

- The provider understands the property and deadline
- Scope and exclusions are written down
- Price and payment terms are clear
- Insurance information is available
- Access arrangements are secure
- Reviews and claims appear credible
- Cancellation and re-clean terms are understood
- You know who to contact on the day
