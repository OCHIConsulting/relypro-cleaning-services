# Service delivery templates

These are internal drafts. Confirm operational details and branding before publishing or sending them.

## Commercial capability statement

RelyPro Cleaning Services provides planned cleaning for offices, managed properties, communal areas, short-stay accommodation and other suitable premises across the published Derby service area. The website currently presents office cleaning, washroom hygiene, carpet cleaning and managed-property services. A site assessment is used to confirm scope, frequency, access, scheduling, hygiene priorities, reporting and escalation before a proposal is issued.

Suggested capability sections: customer/site profile; agreed areas and frequencies; measurable task checklist; mobilisation and access; equipment/material responsibility; quality inspection and issue escalation; service reporting; insurance/compliance documents available on request; named contact; next step (book a site assessment).

Do not add certifications, guarantees, response times, staff-vetting claims or service promises unless operations has verified them.

## Commercial site-assessment checklist

- Organisation, site name, Derby location, decision maker and facilities contact
- Premises type, opening/occupancy hours and proposed cleaning window
- Areas, floor types, approximate dimensions, washrooms and high-touch points
- Required frequency, periodic tasks and measurable acceptance standard
- Access, keys/alarms, lone-working constraints, parking, lifts and waste routes
- Sensitive/restricted areas and information-security rules
- Client-supplied versus RelyPro-supplied consumables/equipment
- Current pain points, incident/escalation path and reporting preference
- Health, safety, COSHH and site-induction requirements
- Start date, procurement process, budget/quote format and next decision date
- Permission status for photographs, testimonial or later case study (default: no)

## Airbnb/short-stay onboarding checklist

- Property reference, type/size, published location area and portfolio owner
- Turnaround window, check-out/check-in times and contingency contact
- Key/access process stored only in the approved secure channel
- Room-by-room turnover checklist and presentation standard
- Linen ownership, sizes, quantities, laundry flow and damaged-item process
- Approved restocking list, par levels, purchasing authority and receipt handling
- Genuine condition photographs: required angles, secure storage, retention and guest-privacy rule
- Issue categories, urgent/non-urgent escalation, spending authority and response owner
- Lost property, damage, maintenance and safety incident handling
- Deep-clean cadence and optional carpet/oven requirements
- First-turn quality review, owner sign-off and recurring review date
