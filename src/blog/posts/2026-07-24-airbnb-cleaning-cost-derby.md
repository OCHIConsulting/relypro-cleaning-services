Airbnb cleaning prices are easiest to compare when every quote covers the same work. A low headline price may exclude linen, restocking, multiple bathrooms or the time needed for a guest-ready inspection.

## What a standard turnover may include

An agreed RelyPro turnover can cover:

- Kitchen and bathroom cleaning
- Dusting accessible surfaces
- Vacuuming and mopping
- Waste removal
- Bed making with supplied clean linen
- Towel reset
- Restocking agreed consumables
- Reporting visible damage or shortages
- Final presentation check

The exact checklist is property-specific. Laundry processing, purchasing stock, carpet extraction, oven deep cleaning and excessive-condition work may require separate pricing.

## Property size is only the starting point

Bedrooms help indicate workload, but these factors also matter:

- Number of bathrooms
- Guest capacity and bed setup
- Sofa beds
- Floor area and number of levels
- Carpet versus hard flooring
- Kitchen size
- Parking and building access
- Lift availability
- Checkout-to-check-in window

A compact two-bedroom apartment with one bathroom may take less time than a two-bedroom duplex with two bathrooms, a sofa bed and distant parking.

## Linen changes the operation

Clarify whether:

- Clean linen is already at the property
- The cleaner makes beds only
- Used linen is bagged for collection
- Laundry is washed and dried on-site
- A laundry provider collects and returns
- Linen hire is included

On-site laundry can extend the turnover and introduce drying risk. Our [Airbnb linen management guide](/blog/airbnb-linen-management-guide/) explains why several complete usable sets improve resilience.

## Restocking should be priced transparently

Restocking can mean:

1. Moving host-supplied items from a locked cupboard into the guest area.
2. Counting stock and reporting shortages.
3. Purchasing approved items within a spending limit.
4. Managing regular bulk orders.

These are different levels of responsibility. Agree product costs, purchase time, receipts, substitutions and minimum quantities.

Use our [Airbnb restocking checklist](/blog/airbnb-restocking-checklist/) to define what is presented and what remains in reserve.

## Condition matters

Turnover pricing normally assumes the property has been used in an ordinary way. Extra work may be required for:

- Excessive rubbish
- Heavy grease
- Large spills
- Parties or unapproved events
- Significant pet hair
- Stained linen
- Maintenance contamination
- Late checkout reducing available time

Agree how the cleaner documents and authorises additional work. The host should receive evidence and make booking decisions rather than the team silently rushing.

## Periodic work is different from turnover work

Routine changeovers should not carry every deep-cleaning task. Plan separate or rotational work for:

- Oven interiors
- Carpet extraction
- Upholstery cleaning
- Behind large furniture
- Internal windows
- Heavy limescale
- Cupboard inventory

Our [Airbnb deep-cleaning schedule](/blog/airbnb-deep-cleaning-schedule/) shows how to prevent these details building up.

## Compare quotes fairly

Ask each provider:

- Is the price per turnover, hour or cleaner-hour?
- Which rooms and tasks are included?
- Are products and equipment supplied?
- Is linen processing included?
- What restocking work is included?
- Are photographs or reports provided?
- What counts as excessive condition?
- Is there a minimum term or booking volume?
- What are cancellation and access-failure terms?
- Is cover available when the usual cleaner is absent?

Price matters, but missed check-ins and inconsistent standards have operational costs too.

## Give accurate information for a quote

Send:

- Full postcode
- Bedrooms and bathrooms
- Bed sizes and guest capacity
- Floor area or listing link
- Normal checkout and check-in times
- Access and parking details
- Linen process
- Restocking list
- Booking frequency
- Photographs or walkthrough if requested

The more accurately the normal turnover is defined, the more reliable the price and schedule can be.
