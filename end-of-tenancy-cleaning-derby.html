Cleaning built around the property handover

End-of-tenancy requirements vary between properties and agents. We ask for the inventory or preferred checklist where available, then confirm what is included before the appointment. This creates a clear record for tenants, landlords and property managers.

Kitchen

Cupboards, worktops, sinks, splashbacks, appliance exteriors and accessible fittings. Oven and internal appliance cleaning are quoted as selected extras.

Bathrooms

Sanitaryware, showers, screens, tiles, mirrors, taps and accessible surfaces, with attention to residue and limescale.

Rooms and storage

Empty-room surfaces, cupboard interiors where agreed, skirting boards, doors, handles, reachable ledges and floors.

Optional extras

Carpet extraction, upholstery, oven detailing, internal windows and other specialist tasks can be added when suitable.

Preparing for the appointment

The property should normally be empty of personal belongings, with utilities connected and safe access available. We need advance notice of heavy waste, damage, mould, pest contamination or hazardous materials because these are outside a standard handover clean and may require a specialist.

Guide prices: studio from £135, one-bedroom from £175 and two-bedroom from £225. Oven cleaning, carpet extraction and other specialist extras are quoted separately. The written quote depends on bathrooms, floor area, condition and selected extras. A clean cannot guarantee return of a tenancy deposit because agents may make deductions for damage or matters outside cleaning.

Useful information to send

  • Property postcode and size
  • Move-out or handover date
  • Inventory or agent checklist
  • Photos of heavier areas
  • Required oven or carpet work
