A cleaning schedule matched to your workplace

Offices differ in footfall, layout, operating hours and hygiene needs. We start with the number of workstations, washrooms, kitchens, meeting spaces and public areas, then agree a task schedule and visit frequency. One-off, daily and recurring arrangements are available subject to capacity.

Work areas

Accessible desks and surfaces, meeting rooms, touchpoints, bins and floors, following agreed clear-desk and access rules.

Kitchens and break areas

Worktops, sinks, cupboard fronts, appliance exteriors, tables and floors, with replenishment tasks agreed separately.

Washrooms

Sanitaryware, mirrors, basins, high-touch points and floors, with a routine based on occupancy and required frequency.

Floors and shared areas

Vacuuming, mopping and spot cleaning for receptions, corridors and shared spaces, using methods appropriate to each floor.

Site visit and written scope

For recurring or larger commercial work, a site visit helps confirm access, alarms, keys, waste handling, storage, lone-working requirements and any restricted areas. The quote records the tasks, frequency, service window and who provides consumables.

Commercial pricing: tailored to the site, visit frequency and agreed service level following a free site assessment. Your written quote records the tasks, schedule and price before work begins.
